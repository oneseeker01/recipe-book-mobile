## 🔄 **Manual Update Trigger (If Auto-Update Doesn't Work)**

### **Clear App Cache Method:**

1. **Go to Android Settings** → **Apps** → **Recipe Book**
2. **Tap "Storage"** → **Clear Cache**
3. **Clear data** (optional, but safe)
4. **Reopen app** - update will be forced

### **Force Restart Method:**

1. **Long press** Recipe Book app icon
2. **Tap "Force Stop"**
3. **Reopen app** - update downloads

## 🛠️ **Troubleshooting Updates**

### **If Update Doesn't Download:**

1. **Check internet connection**
2. **Clear app cache** (steps above)
3. **Force close and reopen**
4. **Restart phone** if needed

### **If Update Fails:**

- **App will work normally** - no breaking changes
- **Update can be retried** next time you open app
